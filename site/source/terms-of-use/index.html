1. Acceptance of Terms

This site describes Roomly, a property management application, and links to a working demo of it. Using either means accepting what follows.

2. Use of the Service

Demo access is given on request rather than published, and the data inside a demo account is fictional and periodically rebuilt, so nothing done in a demo persists in any meaningful way and nothing in it belongs to a real person.

3. User Accounts and Responsibilities

There is no public sign-up, and accounts are not self-service. Anyone running their own deployment of Roomly is responsible for their own accounts, for their tenants' data, and for their own obligations as a landlord or agent.
