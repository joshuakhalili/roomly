A morning with Roomly

Open it, see what needs you, get on with your day.

  1. 01

    The next thing to do, first

    The dashboard ranks what is open the way an experienced manager would: money owed, then legal deadlines, then paperwork, then empty rooms. The top one is a single click away.

  2. 02

    Rent, and who is behind on it

    Weekly, fortnightly, monthly or one total for a short stay. Late payments group by tenant, and a reminder opens in WhatsApp or copies for WeChat, written in the language that tenant reads.

  3. 03

    Certificates that never quietly lapse

    Gas safety, electrical, EPC and fire records for every building on one grid, with expiry countdowns. Deposit protection is tracked against its 30-day deadline from the day a tenant moves in.

  4. 04

    The condition of a room, proven

    Walk each room section by section, rating condition and cleanliness with dated photos. Do it again at check-out and Roomly lays the two side by side, so a deposit conversation is about evidence.

Questions

The questions a landlord asks first.

Is Roomly a full property management system?

Roomly covers the day-to-day of letting rooms in shared houses, HMOs and other room-by-room properties: tenancies and occupants, rent, an expense ledger, documents, maintenance, safety certificates and condition reports. It totals spend by UK tax year so an accountant can pick it up, but it is not accounting software and it does not file your return.

Who is Roomly designed for?

Small UK letting operations that let rooms individually rather than whole flats: shared houses, licensed HMOs, boutique buildings, or a landlord running a mix of long lets and short stays. It fits below the size where agency software starts to make sense, and it is built around the room rather than the building, so properties of any shape work the same way.

How does the inventory report work?

Each room type has its own numbered sections, and each section carries a condition rating, a cleanliness rating, notes and any number of dated photos. Check-in and check-out are the same report recorded twice, so the comparison shows exactly what changed.

Does Roomly handle short stays as well as long tenancies?

Yes. A short stay is the same tenancy record as a long let, priced as one total instead of a repeating charge. Longer tenancies run on weekly, fortnightly, four-weekly or monthly rent, with month-end dates clamped rather than allowed to drift, so a tenant who moves in on the 31st is billed on the 28th in February and back on the 31st in March.

How do reminders actually reach a phone?

Each admin gets a private calendar subscription, so move-ins, move-outs and rent dates land in their own phone calendar with alarms attached. Tenant reminders go out as one-click WhatsApp messages, pre-filled in the language that tenant reads.

What happens to a tenant's identity documents?

Passports and right-to-rent checks sit in private storage, served through short-lived links, and are deleted a year after the tenancy ends, which is what the Home Office guidance asks for. Money and tenancy records are kept for six years.
